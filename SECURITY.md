# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately rather than opening a public issue.

- GitHub: use **Report a vulnerability** under the repository's Security tab
  (Private vulnerability reporting).
- Email: <urevich55@gmail.com>

Please include a description of the issue, the affected version or commit, and
steps to reproduce. A proof of concept is welcome but not required.

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few days. We follow coordinated
disclosure: we ask that details stay private until a fix is released, and we are
happy to credit you in the advisory and to request a CVE on your behalf, or to
support your own CVE request.
